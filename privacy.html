Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Purpose

Hermes rclone Drive is a personal project using rclone to manage cloud storage and backups through Google Drive.

Google Drive Permissions

With the account owner's authorization, rclone may list, read, upload, modify and delete Google Drive files as directed by the owner.

Data and Credentials

OAuth tokens are stored in rclone configuration files on devices controlled by the owner, not on this website.

This website does not collect Google Drive files, passwords or OAuth tokens.

Storage and Sharing

Files are transferred only between storage locations selected by the owner. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Revoking Access

The owner may revoke Google Drive access through Google Account security settings and remove locally stored credentials.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
